Four service areas

Improve the system, the process, or the connection between them.

Every engagement is shaped around a defined operational need; services can be combined where the problem crosses functions.

01 / FOUNDATION

ERP & Business Systems Implementation

The business problem

As a business grows, teams may outgrow disconnected tools or find the same operational information scattered across spreadsheets, email, and standalone software.

What Takumi implements

Map the workflows and information needs, support platform selection or configuration where in scope, structure records and roles, and prepare the connected processes, reports, and user guidance.

Grounded use case

For a wholesale distributor, an illustrative design could connect customer orders, inventory allocation, purchasing exceptions, and invoicing through an ERP-led workflow.

Intended operational outcome

A more consistent place to manage core business records and workflows, with clearer ownership and fewer disconnected handoffs. The specific result depends on platform fit, data quality, and adoption.

02 / IMPROVEMENT

Business Process Optimization

The business problem

Unclear responsibilities, repeated approvals, and workarounds can make routine processes slower or difficult to hand over consistently.

What Takumi implements

Document the current process, identify friction and decision points, agree a practical future-state workflow, clarify owners and exceptions, and align supporting tools and procedures.

Grounded use case

Map a purchasing approval from request through authorization, order placement, receipt, and invoice matching; clarify who acts at each point and what information is needed.

Intended operational outcome

A more understandable and repeatable process, with visible handoffs and clearer exception paths that the team can review and refine.

03 / CONNECTION

Systems Integration

The business problem

When applications do not exchange information reliably, teams may re-key the same data, reconcile versions manually, or miss updates between tools.

What Takumi implements

Map the systems and data flow, define the source of truth and synchronization rules, configure suitable connections, and plan monitoring, error handling, and ownership.

Grounded use case

Connect an online order source with an ERP and fulfilment process so order details and status changes can be shared according to agreed rules.

Intended operational outcome

Less duplicate transfer for the connected workflow and a clearer view of where information comes from, where it goes, and who resolves a failed handoff.

04 / AUTOMATION

Intelligent Workflow Automation

The business problem

Routine tasks, reminders, and predictable handoffs can take attention away from work that needs human judgment, especially when the process relies on copying information between tools.

What Takumi implements

Identify suitable repeatable steps, define triggers and decision rules, automate agreed actions, retain human review for exceptions, and document monitoring and ownership.

Grounded use case

Route an order exception to the appropriate owner with the relevant context and a clear follow-up task, leaving the final decision with a person where needed.

Intended operational outcome

Routine steps can become more consistent and visible, while exception handling remains deliberate. No time-saved or financial result is assumed.
